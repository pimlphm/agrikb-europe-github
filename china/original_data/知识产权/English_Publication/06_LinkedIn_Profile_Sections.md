## Experience Section — Project Entry

### AeroKG: Civil Aircraft Airworthiness Knowledge Graph System
**Role:** Creator & Lead Developer
**Date:** 2025 – Present

Designed and developed an AI-powered knowledge graph system for civil aircraft airworthiness certification, combining industrial ontology (IOF/BFO) with large language model technology.

Key achievements:
- Defined a 74-type, 36-relation aerospace ontology aligned with ARP4754A, DO-178C, and ARP4761 standards
- Invented a seven-level association strength spectrum (Red-to-Violet) for quantified knowledge visualization
- Built six specialized LLM agent roles for automated safety assessment, traceability verification, and change impact analysis
- Integrated FAA, EASA, and CAAC standards with automatic cross-referencing (23 standards)
- Designed an experience accumulation engine with per-program management and bilingual audit capabilities
- Filed a Chinese invention patent (10 claims) and registered software copyright

Technologies: Python, PyQt5, RDFLib, NetworkX, Ollama, vLLM-Ascend (Huawei Ascend 910B NPU)
